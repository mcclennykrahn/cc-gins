## Exact colored measurement budget

Code evidence: `ccgins/sim.py:198-206` draws independent white noise with per-axis sigma_h1m/sigma_v2m plus AR(1) multipath mp_sigma.5m, tau30s. MP starts at zero at the first GNSS epoch (1s), and continues evolving through invalid epochs; it is neither stationary initially nor reset by an outage. `ccgins/pipeline.py:179` supplies nominal-white diagonal R to the filter; `ccgins/filters.py:282-286` uses antenna position Jacobian. Colored MP is an acknowledged model/calibration limitation, not a newly proven implementation bug.

For generated epochs t_i, let u_i=t_i-t_0. Exact one-axis residual covariance is C_ij = sigma² delta_ij + mp_sigma²[exp(-|u_i-u_j|/tau)-exp(-(u_i+u_j)/tau)]. Subselect available epochs only after constructing C, preserving correlation across missing fixes. The diagnostic reports mean variance 1ᵀC1/n², marginal variance trace(C)/n, and effective n=(trace(C)/n)/(1ᵀC1/n²). Effective n is defined for this unweighted residual mean; it is not a navigation information count or filter innovation statistic.

Measurement residual histories themselves are generated by the real simulator for each sensor seed. Per-axis marginal and mean variances, vertical results, counts, measured means and signed antenna residual traces reside in colored/. Stationary GNSS-only radial H RMS is sqrt(2*(1²+.5²))=1.581m, V sqrt(2²+.5²)=2.062m; finite traces need not equal these expectations.

The61-fix H-axis exact mean variance .118976m² is7.258 times the nominal-white prediction and5.844 times the independent-inflated prediction. A30-fix block retains only about6 independent-fix equivalents for this residual-mean statistic. Correlation therefore matters for uncertainty of slow position offsets even though marginal MP variance is modest. A scalar R inflation cannot reproduce this covariance. This evidence alone does not identify online covariance inconsistency, establish that MP causes outage drift, or justify adding states without observability tests.
